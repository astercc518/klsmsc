Privacy

This domain operates as a link redirection endpoint. When a short URL is opened, the service performs an HTTP 302 redirect to the destination chosen by the link's sender.

What we record

For each redirect we log a click counter, a coarse client IP fragment, and the User-Agent string for the sole purpose of fraud and abuse detection. No personal identifiers, cookies, or persistent device fingerprints are collected.

Third-party destinations

We do not host or control the content of destination pages. Please review the privacy policy of the destination site for further information.

Data retention

Aggregate click metrics are retained for up to 90 days, after which they are purged from active storage.

Contact

For privacy-related requests, please reach out via the contact channel posted by the sender of the message that brought you here.
